This is a plan, committed on 14 October 2026 before the run it reads has
started. That run, `prefill.sh` in `~/.aura/rlc-evidence/g09-runs`, waits
behind attempt 2 and G12 for the model.

## Why a third plan now

Attempt 2 (G09_PROCEDURES_PREREGISTRATION_2_2026-10-12.md) has not finished.
If its breadth criterion fails, a third attempt needs fresh held-out kinds and
an induction run over them. The prefill run induces procedures for 97 kinds
from their development halves, with attempt 2's settings, to fill her book.
Fixed before that run writes anything, it serves as attempt 3's held-out
induction for the kinds below.

Attempt 3 is read only if attempt 2 does not close G09. If attempt 2 closes
it, these numbers are reported as the prefill's measurement and decide
nothing.

`tools/induce_g09_procedures.py` at 89826b8fc, `--kinds all` except the 16

## Held-out kinds

* math: the 52 DeepMind mathematics modules attempt 2 did not draw
  (interpolation set, Saxton et al. 2019);
* transfer: the 27 BIG-Bench Hard tasks, each posed with BBH's three worked
  examples, as the transfer domain poses them.

Each kind's problems are split in halves by a hash of their id; induction
sees only the first half.

In her book but not held out: 17 BBEH tasks and Natural Plan calendar,
whose test halves were used in attempt 1. Requests they answer count toward
no harm.

What these kinds have already touched: four DeepMind modules were drawn for
attempt 2, and the other 52 have never been posed to her. BBH questions were
drafted in the G09 pilots and are among attempt 2's routing-only requests;
no procedure for a BBH task has been induced or chosen, and no decision
about induction used them. DeepMind mathematics and several BBH tasks (Dyck
languages, word sorting, Boolean expressions, multistep arithmetic) are
generated by programs; the result lists gains by kind so a reader can see
how much of a domain's gain comes from those.

## Book and requests

The book is the prefill run's book. Attempt 2's 16 kinds join it as
signatures only, so a request nearest one of them is not answered.

For each held-out domain, 300 requests from the test halves of its kinds
that keep a procedure, divided as evenly as possible among those kinds (the
remainder to the first kinds in `random.Random(20261014)` order), and drawn
by `random.Random(20261014)`; a kind with fewer test problems than its share
gives all of them. Every request of the routing-only sets (MATH outside
MATH-500, AIME 2024 and 2025, HumanEval+, HotpotQA, BBEH Mini) is routed
through the book too. A draft is decoded for every request a kept procedure
answers, with attempt 2's drafting settings; a request no kept procedure
answers keeps its draft in both arms and cannot be discordant.

Domains: math = the DeepMind kinds, MATH and AIME; transfer = BBH and BBEH
Mini; code = HumanEval+; knowledge = HotpotQA.

## Claims, fixed before the run

This is the third attempt at one item, so both claims that can close it are
tested at 0.05 / 3 = 0.0167 (Bonferroni over the attempts).

* Primary: pooled over the domains, kept answers gain more requests than
  they lose; exact two-sided McNemar, alpha 0.0167.
* Breadth: in math and in transfer, counting held-out kinds only, gains
  exceed losses at two-sided alpha 0.0167 within the domain.
* No harm: no domain has losses exceeding gains at two-sided alpha 0.05,
  counting every request a kept procedure answered in that domain, held out
  or not.
* G09 closes on attempt 3 only if all three hold.

With no losses, seven gains reject at 0.0167 (two-sided exact p = 0.0156).
At a 5% draft error rate, 300 requests give about 15 expected gains.
