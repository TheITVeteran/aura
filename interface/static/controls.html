What am I looking at?

These controls define external boundaries. Safe mode is the big red brake — flip it and she can't change files or act in the outside world until you turn it back. It does not suspend her thought, identity, learning, or agency. The sensor switches decide what the operating system allows her to see and hear. Every change is saved and applied right away.
